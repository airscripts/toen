# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and precise. Welcome
different backgrounds, critique ideas rather than people, and keep linguistic
examples contextual and non-harassing.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed confidentially and handled with care. Maintainers may remove
contributions or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project
safe and productive.
