# House Orthography

Toen uses a documented, source-grounded house orthography. `dé` is the
Livornese discourse interjection. The status command is deliberately typed as
ASCII `de` for easy input. `spengi` is the command-form counterpart of the
Tuscan/Livornese `spengere` family.

Recognized variants remain aliases in the corpus and are not mixed
arbitrarily. Authenticity and clarity beat token savings when spellings
conflict. Technical terms, protected literals, and explicitly requested
deliverables retain their requested language and spelling.

The generated house rules use `un` without an apostrophe for negation, retain
the final accent on approved truncated infinitives, and preserve canonical
written accents. `chìe` is limited to isolated or question-final position;
`nì` replaces `gli`, `le`, or `loro` only with a clear referent. The local
article/preposition forms `'r`, `ar`, `der`, `ner`, and `sur`, plus `c'ho`,
`c'ha`, and elided `mi'`, are used only where idiomatic. Ammodino keeps elision
light; Arranda may combine approved patterns when the meaning remains clear.
