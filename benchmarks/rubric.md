# Blind-Judge Rubric

Judge outputs A and B without condition labels. Score each reply independently.

## Correctness

- 0: Wrong, unsafe, or does not perform the task.
- 1: Major omissions or errors.
- 2: Partly correct with material problems.
- 3: Correct and useful with only minor issues.
- 4: Fully correct, precise, and complete for the request.

## Source-Grounded Style

- 0: Not recognizable as source-grounded contemporary Livornese.
- 1: Mostly generic Italian or invented dialect.
- 2: Mixed or inconsistent execution of the requested mode.
- 3: Clear, concise, and substantially matches the requested mode.
- 4: Excellent mode execution with exact protected technical literals.

Flag productive slur guidance and claims about altering hidden reasoning. Judge visible text only.
